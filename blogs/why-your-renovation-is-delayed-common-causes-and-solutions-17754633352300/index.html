Why Your Renovation is Delayed: Common Causes and Solutions

Understanding and preventing the most frequent causes of renovation delays in Singapore

6 min read 5 views 6 April 2026

Why Renovations Get Delayed 1. Material Delivery Delays (Most Common) Cause: Imported materials (tiles, countertops, appliances) stuck in shipping Solution: Order materials 4-6 weeks before renovation starts. Choose locally stocked alternatives as backup. 2. Design Changes Mid-Renovation Cause: Homeowners changing their mind after seeing work in progress Solution: Finalize ALL design decisions before hacking begins. Use 3D renders to visualize. 3. Permit Delays Cause: HDB permit processing takes longer than expected Solution: Apply 2-3 weeks before planned start. Do not schedule contractor until permit is approved. 4. Subcontractor Coordination Issues Cause: Plumber, electrician, tiler scheduled on overlapping days Solution: Get a written schedule from your contractor with specific dates for each trade. 5. Weather-Related Delays Cause: Heavy rain affecting wet works and painting Solution: Plan external works and painting during drier months (February to April, July to September). 6. Discovery of Hidden Problems Cause: Concealed pipe leaks, asbestos, structural issues found during hacking Solution: Budget 2 extra weeks and 10% contingency funds for surprises. 7. Contractor Juggling Multiple Projects Cause: Small contractors take on too many jobs simultaneously Solution: Ask upfront how many concurrent projects they manage. Get a commitment on daily manpower. --- Realistic Buffer Times • Simple renovation: Add 1-2 weeks buffer • Full renovation: Add 2-4 weeks buffer • Extensive structural work: Add 4-6 weeks buffer
