Feature Walls: Ideas, Materials, and Cost Guide

Creating a stunning focal point in your living room or bedroom without breaking the bank

5 min read 2 views 6 April 2026

Feature Wall Guide for Singapore Homes A feature wall creates a focal point in a room and adds character to an otherwise plain space. It is one of the most popular design elements in Singapore renovations. Popular Feature Wall Styles Fluted panel walls use vertical ridged panels to create texture and depth. They are the top trending feature wall in 2025-2026. Available in wood, MDF, and PVC. Cost: $30-80 per sqft installed. Wood slat walls use horizontal or vertical timber strips mounted on a backing panel. They add warmth and a natural feel. Cost: $25-60 per sqft. Textured paint finishes like limewash, microcement, or venetian plaster create an artisanal, organic look. Cost: $15-40 per sqft. Stone or brick veneer cladding adds an industrial or rustic feel. Use lightweight veneer sheets rather than real brick to save weight and cost. Cost: $20-50 per sqft. Wallpaper is the most affordable option. Modern wallpapers are durable and come in endless designs. Cost: $5-20 per sqft installed. Built-in TV console walls combine storage, display, and entertainment in one cohesive unit. They hide cables and provide a clean look. Cost: $2,000-6,000 for a full wall unit. Where to Place a Feature Wall Behind the TV in the living room is the most common location. It draws the eye and anchors the seating arrangement. Behind the bed headboard in the master bedroom creates a hotel-like atmosphere. The dining area wall can feature an accent that defines the dining zone in an open-concept layout. The entryway or foyer sets the tone for the entire home from the moment you walk in. Design Rules Only one feature wall per room. Multiple accent walls compete for attention and create visual chaos. Keep the other walls neutral (white, light grey) to let the feature wall stand out. Consider lighting. A feature wall looks best with accent lighting such as cove lights above or spotlights directed at it. The feature wall should be visible from the main sitting position in the room. Budget-Friendly Options A single wall of bold paint color costs under $100 in materials. This is the cheapest feature wall option. Peel-and-stick wood panels ($8-15 per sqft) install without any renovation work and can be removed without damage. Gallery walls using framed prints and photos create a personalized feature without renovation. Cost depends on frames and prints but can be as low as $100-300. Large format artwork (a single big piece) creates impact without any wall modification.
