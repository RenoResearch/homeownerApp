Curtains and Blinds Guide for Singapore Homes

Comparing different window treatments and choosing the right option for each room

5 min read 3 views 6 April 2026

Window Treatments for Singapore Homes The right curtains or blinds control light, provide privacy, and significantly impact your room's appearance. Singapore's intense sunlight makes this choice particularly important. Day and Night Curtains The most popular option in Singapore homes. A sheer day curtain filters sunlight while maintaining an airy feel. A thicker night curtain provides darkness for sleeping and privacy. Both hang on a double track system. Day curtain fabrics include voile, organza, and linen blends. Night curtain fabrics include blackout polyester, velvet, and cotton blends. Cost: $8-25 per foot for day and night curtains combined, including track and installation. Roller Blinds Clean, modern look that suits minimalist interiors. Available in various opacity levels from sheer to full blackout. Motorized options available for hard-to-reach windows. Easy to maintain. Simply wipe with a damp cloth. No washing needed. Cost: $80-250 per window depending on size and motorization. Venetian Blinds Available in aluminium, wood, and faux wood. Allow precise control of light angle. Can be tilted to let light in while maintaining privacy. Aluminium venetians are affordable ($50-150 per window) but can look industrial. Wood venetians ($150-400) add warmth but real wood can warp in humidity. Faux wood ($100-300) offers the look of wood without the moisture problems. Zebra Blinds Also called combi blinds. Alternating sheer and opaque horizontal bands that can be aligned for different levels of light and privacy. A modern alternative to traditional venetians. Cost: $100-300 per window. Room-by-Room Recommendations Living room: Day and night curtains for versatility and a soft, furnished look. Bedrooms: Blackout roller blinds or thick night curtains for quality sleep. Singapore's early sunrise (around 7 AM) makes blackout essential for late sleepers. Kitchen: Roller blinds or venetian blinds that are easy to wipe clean. Avoid fabric curtains near the stove. Bathroom: Waterproof roller blinds or frosted window film for privacy. Study: Venetian or zebra blinds for adjustable light control during screen work. Measurement Tips Measure the window recess width and height. For curtains, add 15-20cm on each side for full coverage. For blinds inside the recess, subtract 1cm for clearance. Always have your supplier do the final measurement before ordering.
