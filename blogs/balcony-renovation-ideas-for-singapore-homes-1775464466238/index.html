Balcony Renovation Ideas for Singapore Homes

Transform your balcony into a functional outdoor living space

5 min read 0 views 6 April 2026

Making the Most of Your Balcony Many Singapore condos and some HDB flats come with balconies that are underutilized. With thoughtful design, this space can become your favorite spot at home. Flooring Options Outdoor deck tiles (wood or composite) can be laid over existing balcony tiles without any renovation. They are modular and removable. Cost: $5-15 per sqft. Composite decking is more durable than real wood in Singapore's weather. Artificial grass gives a garden feel and is soft underfoot. Choose UV-resistant varieties that will not fade in Singapore's sun. Cost: $3-8 per sqft. Outdoor rugs are the simplest option. Choose weather-resistant materials that dry quickly. Replace every 1-2 years. Furniture for Small Balconies A bistro set (small round table with two folding chairs) fits balconies as narrow as 1.5m wide. Look for aluminium or treated teak that withstands outdoor conditions. A bench with storage underneath serves double duty. Use it for seating and store gardening tools or cushions inside. A hanging egg chair is a popular choice if your balcony can support wall mounting. Freestanding versions with their own frame also work. Avoid heavy furniture that blocks the view and makes the space feel cramped. Plants and Greenery Balcony gardens thrive in Singapore's climate. Choose plants that tolerate partial shade and direct sun depending on your balcony orientation. Good choices include pothos, monstera, snake plant, peace lily for shaded balconies. For sunny balconies, bougainvillea, herbs like basil and mint, and succulents do well. Use vertical planters and wall-mounted pots to maximize growing space without taking up floor area. Ensure your balcony drain is not blocked by soil or fallen leaves. Regular cleaning prevents water pooling. Weather Protection A retractable awning provides shade and rain protection. Check with your condo management before installing. Outdoor blinds or bamboo roll-up screens provide privacy and wind protection. For HDB balconies, check current regulations before enclosing or installing permanent fixtures.
