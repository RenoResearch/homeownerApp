BTO Renovation Budget Breakdown: How to Spend S$35K to S$60K Wisely in 2026

Collecting your BTO keys is exciting, but the renovation bill can feel daunting. Here is a realistic breakdown of where your money goes for a 4-room BTO flat in 2026, so you can plan with clarity.

5 min read 0 views 31 March 2026

Why BTO Renovations Are More Affordable BTO flats arrive with brand-new plumbing, electrical wiring, and waterproofing. You do not need to hack old tiles, replace rusty pipes, or rewire the entire unit. This saves you S$10,000 to S$20,000 compared to a resale flat of the same size. Cost Breakdown for a 4-Room BTO (Mid-Range) Carpentry (wardrobes, kitchen cabinets, shoe cabinet, TV console): S$15,000 to S$22,000. This is your single largest expense. Custom carpentry using plywood with laminate finishes is the standard in Singapore. Kitchen (overlay tiling, backsplash, countertop, sink, hob, hood): S$5,000 to S$8,000. Most BTO owners overlay tiles rather than hack, keeping costs manageable. Bathrooms x2 (vanity, shower set, mirror cabinet, accessories): S$4,000 to S$7,000. With new waterproofing already in place, you only need fixtures and fittings. Electrical and Lighting (additional power points, cove lights, downlights): S$3,000 to S$5,000. Budget S$70 to S$100 per additional power point and S$80 to S$150 per downlight. Flooring (vinyl throughout bedrooms and living room): S$2,500 to S$4,500. Vinyl is the most popular BTO flooring choice for its cost-effectiveness and ease of maintenance. Painting (entire unit, 2 to 3 colours): S$1,500 to S$2,500. Design Fee and Project Management: S$2,000 to S$4,000. Many ID firms bundle this into the overall package. Contingency (10 to 15 percent): S$3,500 to S$6,000. Watch Out: Air conditioning, curtains, and appliances are typically excluded from renovation packages. Budget an additional S$5,000 to S$10,000 for these items. Where to Save Without Sacrificing Quality Choose vinyl flooring over tiles for bedrooms. The cost difference is significant and vinyl is easier to replace years later. Opt for laminate countertops instead of quartz if budget is tight. The savings of S$1,000 to S$2,000 can go toward better carpentry hardware. Skip the feature wall. A clean, well-painted wall with good lighting achieves 80 percent of the visual impact at 20 percent of the cost.
