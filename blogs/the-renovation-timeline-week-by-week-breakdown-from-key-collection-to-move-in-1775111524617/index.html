The Renovation Timeline: Week-by-Week Breakdown from Key Collection to Move-In

Knowing what happens at each stage of the renovation helps you plan your temporary accommodation, schedule deliveries, and avoid bottlenecks. Here is a realistic week-by-week timeline for a 4-room HDB flat renovation

5 min read 0 views 2 April 2026

Weeks 1 to 2: Preparation and Permits Submit HDB renovation permit application (approved within 2 to 3 business days). Confirm materials, colours, and carpentry dimensions with your designer. Set up site protection (floor coverings, door protection). Begin hacking works (resale flats) or preparation of surfaces (BTO flats). Weeks 3 to 4: Structural and Wet Works Complete hacking and debris removal. Electrical rewiring and new distribution board installation. Plumbing modifications and new pipe runs. Waterproofing of bathrooms and kitchen (including 48-hour ponding test). Masonry work (new partition walls if any). Weeks 5 to 6: Tiling and Flooring Bathroom and kitchen floor and wall tiling. Living room and bedroom flooring installation. Grout application and curing time. This phase requires patience as tiles need time to set properly before heavy traffic. Weeks 7 to 8: Carpentry and Painting Built-in wardrobe installation. Kitchen cabinet installation. TV console, shoe cabinet, and other custom carpentry. Painting of walls and ceilings (2 coats minimum). This is when the space starts to look like a home. Weeks 9 to 10: Fixtures, Fittings, and Finishing Electrical fixture installation (lights, switches, power points). Sanitary fixture installation (taps, toilet bowl, shower, vanity). Door hardware installation. Final touch-ups, cleaning, and defect inspection. Week 10 to 11: Defect Inspection and Handover Conduct a thorough walkthrough with your designer. Check all carpentry (doors, drawers, hinges). Test all electrical points and switches. Run all taps and check for leaks. Inspect paintwork under natural and artificial light. Document any defects for rectification. Once defects are resolved, sign the handover document.
