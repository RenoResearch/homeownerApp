I engaged AC Vision after meeting several interior designers, and my experience with Jonathan and Nikki has been largely positive. From the start, they were transparent and professional — clearly explaining their package and contract, and providing a detailed, line-by-line quotation that laid out the costs openly. Even before I committed, they prepared a draft 3D design proposal, which many other firms wouldn’t do at that stage. They were accommodating with changes and gave helpful suggestions, especially around color combinations and layout ideas. Revisions were handled smoothly. When comparing quotes with other IDs and what my friends paid for similar units, I found AC Vision’s pricing to be reasonable. While the initial quote might seem slightly higher, their CaseTrust accreditation and Jonathan’s honest, transparent communication gave me confidence. Workmanship and quality were good — they used materials such as solid plywood with ABS trimming, which some other firms may charge extra for with a lower upfront cost. That said, there were a couple of hiccups. One of my ceiling lights went missing during the process. I also noticed two of the workers threw cigarette butts into my balcony area. However, Nikki and Johnathan acknowledged and took ownership of the issue, apologized and compensated the missing ceiling light. While the main renovation works were done properly, there’s some room for improvement in enforcing site discipline among workers. All in all, I’d still recommend AC Vision Design for their professionalism, transparency, and willingness to take ownership. Just be firm on site supervision and housekeeping expectations.
