Ali Hassan★★★★★
This renovation strikes a very confident balance between warmth, functionality and modern elegance. The overall palette soft beige walls, warm wood cabinetry and subtle marble textures creates a cohesive, calming atmosphere that feels both upscale and livable. The living area is particularly well executed. The TV feature wall with the marble backdrop and integrated shelving adds a strong focal point without overwhelming the space. The concealed lighting along the ceiling perimeter gives a soft ambient glow, enhancing the room’s depth and making it feel more spacious. The choice of a neutral-toned sofa complements the palette nicely, while the styling elements (plants, ceramics, and books) add just enough personality without clutter. The transition into the dining area is seamless. The use of matching materials and tones maintains visual continuity, while the mirror and console setup subtly expand the space and reflect light. The dining setup feels intimate and practical, with thoughtful styling that gives it a welcoming, almost boutique-hotel vibe. The kitchen stands out for its clean, modern layout and smart use of space. The combination of wood cabinetry and light countertops keeps it visually light despite the amount of storage. The integration of appliances and the compact island/bar area shows good planning functional for daily use but also suitable for hosting. It does lean slightly busy with the number of items on the counter, but that’s more styling than design. One of the strongest aspects of this renovation is the lighting design. The layered approach, cove lighting, downlights, and task lighting adds dimension and mood flexibility throughout the home. It elevates what could have been a standard renovation into something more refined. If there’s any critique, it would be that the design plays it quite safe. While very polished, it leans heavily on current trends (wood slats, marble panels, warm neutrals), which may date over time. Introducing one or two bolder elements art pieces, contrasting textures, or a statement color could give the space a more unique identity. Overall, this is a thoughtfully designed, highly cohesive renovation that prioritizes comfort, practicality and understated luxury. It feels like a space that’s easy to live in, yet impressive enough to host in. Thank you Derick for the ideas you proposed. The time we spend to get the ideas came to reality. I wish you all the best for your new adventure.
