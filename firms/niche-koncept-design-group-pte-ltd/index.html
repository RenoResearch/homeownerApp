About

Niche Koncept is a professional interior design consultancy that aims to turn your dream space into reality. Any living space can turn into a modern work of art with the help of an eye for the bigger picture. We will provide you with personalised design consultations to discuss how you can realise your colourful ideas, carefully considering your wants and needs, including your budget, and even make suggestions to complement your ideas. We also offer various parallel services to maximise your space and find matching furniture that lets your vision stand out. We take great pride in seeing your joy when everything comes together harmoniously. After all, Designing Your Perfect Space is our promise.
