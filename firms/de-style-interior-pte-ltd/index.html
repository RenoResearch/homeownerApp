About

De Style® has steadfastly proven our creative spirit and professionalism with Design & Build projects that flawlessly integrate functionality with an aesthetic sense. Since our establishment in 2012, we have gained the attention and earned the respect of a diverse range of customers. This achievement comes from our focus in creating a strong rapport with clients, working closely with them by listening to their ideas and developing concepts based on each unique need. It is with this stark insight that our firm has successfully completed Interior Design & Renovation projects ranging from all types of Landed, Condominium, HDB and Commercial properties. Despite our successes, De Style® constantly endeavors to improve by keeping up with the latest developments and trends in Design & Build and Interior Design & Renovation. That way, like fine wine, we only keep getting better and better with time. At De Style, our extensive experience in modern interior renovation in Singapore has established us as a leading name in the local design industry. Over the years, our team has perfected the art of creative design, seamlessly blending contemporary aesthetics with functional solutions. Our long track record as a renovation company showcases our ability to deliver innovative and stylish interiors while upholding a reputation for meeting deadlines and managing projects with exceptional professionalism. This balance of creativity and efficient project management has solidified De Style’s position as one of the best designers in the nation, trusted for our expertise and reliability in every project we undertake
