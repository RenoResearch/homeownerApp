About

Unique + Interior Design = UNID UNID stands for unique interior design. We are a dedicated team of interior designers & project coordinators, specialising in providing quality and outstanding Interior design services for residential, corporate office & commercial space. UNID Studio listens to clients’ needs, redefine spaces and deliver projects with their own visual identity that tells a unique story. We execute the entire project from initial concept, through technical design, on-site project management, to the finest details of completion. UNID Studio will walk you through the entire process, turning your vision into a reality. We aim to provide our clients with the best experience and we aspire to share our passion with you through our work.
